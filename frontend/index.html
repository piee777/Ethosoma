Overview

From frozen electron microscopy to a living stress physiology

The connectome is the structural fact. The twin is what the structure becomes when time, metabolism, and deprivation act upon it.

The Ethosoma twin begins as the published FlyWire FAFB reconstruction — an AI-segmented, human-proofread wiring diagram of the adult female brain, resolved from whole-brain electron microscopy at 4×4×40 nm. All 139,255 annotated somas are decoded into a hardware-rendered volumetric point cloud organised by neuropil identity and circuit tag.

On this anatomical substrate we mount an in-silico physiology: a six-state ethogram driven by a neuromodulatory population vector (PAM reward dopamine, PPL1 aversive dopamine, octopaminergic arousal, serotonin, sleep pressure, circadian phase). Exogenous environmental stressors — heat, queues, deadlines, inflation, kinship obligations — enter as integrated allostatic load, and the resulting dynamics render as travelling calcium transients across the real circuit modules: optic columns, mushroom-body valence, and central-complex action selection.

The result is a hypothesis in a browser: one heavy hemisphere of neuroanatomy, one thin layer of deprivation economics, and a running account of what a nervous system does when it cannot afford its own stability.

SPATIAL REFERENCE

All coordinates in JRC2018U adult brain space; native FAFB voxel scale 4×4×40 nm maintained end-to-end.

CIRCUIT TAGGING

Nine canonical modules recovered from the connectome: LC/LPLC looming vision, T-series motion columns, PPL1, PAM, giant-fibre, OA-VUM, central complex, gustatory, antennal olfactory.

RENDERING

139,255-GPU-point pipeline with GCaMP-style calcium envelopes, travelling-wave stimuli, and restraint-tuned bloom at 60 fps.

Mushroom Body Calyx

Kenyon cells form a sparse, high-dimensional associative layer. PAM (307 somas) and PPL1 (16 somas) dopaminergic branches invert the valence of each sensory trace — converting a neutral cue into appetitive reinforcement or aversive salience — and consolidate it into long-term memory.

2,196somas

Central Complex

The ellipsoid body forms a heading compass; the fan-shaped body gates action selection; the protocerebral bridge and noduli close the sensorimotor loop. In the twin this is the integration relay — goal-seeking drive, confidence-weighted arbitration, and the restless craving oscillation of the conditioned state.

81,324somas

Optic Lobes

The largest sensory territory of the brain: 66,769 medulla, 14,555 lobula, 3,333 lobula-plate and 7,385 lamina cells process optic flow at columnar resolution, with 42,027 T-series neurons implementing motion detection and looming-threat estimation.

Theoretical Framework

Resource scarcity enters the nervous system as allostatic load

Economic states are not metaphors for stress physiology — they are boundary conditions on it. The twin implements that boundary condition explicitly, as a closed non-linear dynamical system in main.js — every constant empirically anchored to the FAFB-reconstructed circuit.

The twin’s macroscopic trajectory is governed by a forced slow–fast hybrid dynamical system: continuous neuromodulatory relaxation coupled to a non-linear economic phase space exhibiting saddle-node fold bifurcations, path-dependent kindling, and bio-energetic strain.

State 01 · Appetitive Reinforcement · PAM

Foraging & reward pursuit

PAM (307 somas) encodes reward-prediction error. Every appetitive trace raises dopamine, buffers allostatic load, and re-entrenches the foraging policy until depletion tips the balance.

Pathway — PAM → MB calyx → valence inversion → RPE
State 02 · Aversive Withdrawal · PPL1

Threat salience & punishment

PPL1 (16 somas) punishes. Relief is encoded as a transient PPL1 silence; each authorised dose silences the branch again, ratcheting tolerance upward while the avoidance policy strengthens.

Pathway — PPL1 → MB lobes → threat salience → CC
State 03 · Arousal & Freeze · OA-VUM

Quiescence → Metabolic Deprivation Freeze

Octopaminergic arousal drives locomotion. When octopamine dominance outlasts reward depletion and allostatic load passes threshold, the twin latches into economic-quiescence freeze — reserve preserved, opportunity forfeited.

Pathway — OA-VUM → CC → action arbitration → freeze

Systemic strain therefore forces a bifurcation from adaptive foraging to a persistent, trapped state — formalized as Metabolic Deprivation Freeze (Et-Takhyaal) — where energetic conservation is sustained only through progressive allostatic scarring and network dependency.
